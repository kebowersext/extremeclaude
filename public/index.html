Important Training Updates

Partner Program Requirements Update – Revenue & Training

Revenue Threshold Change
The annual revenue threshold for Gold level (Tier A) increases from $250K to $500K. This change will be visible in the partner dashboard starting Apr 1 (FY26 Q4).
Partners can still be promoted to Gold from Q3 to Q4 under the current $250k threshold. The new requirement applies to: Q4 Rebate eligibility, and Program Levelling for FY27.

Technical Certification Requirement (ENCA / ECP)
Technical certifications are required by Jun 30 (end of FY26). Technical Training Requirements for Gold, Diamond, and Diamond Elite – Fabric Track Mandatory.
Compliance is required for: Q4 Rebate payouts, and Program Levelling for FY27.

Sales and Sales Engineering
The Solutions Selling and Solutions Design tracks are specifically for partners focusing on the sales process, from initial discussions to a solution design to meet a customer's needs. Both tracks are free and available as Video on Demand for easy access and consumption.
The first step is an introductory Solutions Selling Associate level course which is the recommended starting point for both tracks. A final exam must be successfully completed at the conclusion of each course in order to achieve the certification.

Technical Training
Extreme Networks Technical Training (Extreme Networks Certified Administrator / Extreme Certified Professional) empowers our partners to build technical expertise, accelerate deployments, and deliver exceptional outcomes.
Each technical track includes 2–4 Knowledge Credential courses and hands-on workshops.
For partner program resources, certification requirements, and the latest updates, visit PartnerEngage. For training and enrolment support, contact extremeacademy@extremenetworks.com.
